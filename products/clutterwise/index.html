Clutterwise

Home inventory & search

You own it. You just can't remember where you put it. Scan your drawers, closets and bins once, then find anything later.

In development

iPhone · coming soon
  • Built for iPhone
  • AI-assisted scanning
  • QR labels for containers

How it works

Scan once. Search forever.

  1. Scan a spot

    Point your camera at a drawer, shelf or bin, or pick a photo. Tell Clutterwise where it is.

  2. Review what it found

    AI reads each item's name, look and any visible text. You edit, keep or delete before anything is saved.

  3. Search for anything

    Search by name, by description, or by what it's for, and see exactly where it lives.

Stay organized

A map of your home

Rooms, locations, containers

File items the way your home is actually arranged: a room, a spot in it, and the box or drawer inside.

QR labels for your bins

Print a label, stick it on a container, and scan it later with Clutterwise or the Camera app to jump straight to what's inside.

Features

Made for real clutter

  • Scan with camera or photo library. Capture a whole drawer at once.
  • Rich item details. Likely names, category, color, shape, material and text on the item.
  • You're in control. Review, edit, keep or delete each item before saving, or add one by hand.
  • Search that understands you. Normal search plus Advanced Search that matches on meaning and storage context.
  • Backup and restore. Export your inventory, with or without photos, and bring it back anytime.
  • Share with your household. Merge in someone else's inventory without wiping your own.
