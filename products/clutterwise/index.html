Clutterwise

Home inventory & search

Know what you own and where it is. Get organized, pack for a move, or run a storage unit. Scan with AI or type it in, then find anything in seconds.

In development

iPhone · coming soon
  • AI scanning or manual entry
  • QR labels for boxes
  • Built for iPhone

Who it's for

One app, a lot of ways to use it

At home

Get organized

Catalog the junk drawer, the closets and the pantry once. Stop buying duplicates and stop hunting for things you know you own.

Moving

Pack with a plan

Label every box with a QR code, fill it in as you pack, and scan it at the other end to see exactly what's inside and where it goes.

Professional movers

Keep every job straight

Label and scan boxes for each customer so crews can tell what's in a box without opening it, and clients get a clear record.

Storage units

Remember what's in storage

Give every bin and shelf a label. Months later, search for the item and know which bin to pull instead of emptying the unit.

Garage & seasonal

Tame the overflow

Holiday decorations, camping gear, tools and sports equipment: know which tote has what before you start digging.

Households

Share the inventory

Merge your inventory with a partner's or roommate's so everyone can find things, without wiping out what either of you has saved.

See it in action

Capture it your way

Scan with AI

Point, shoot, review

Photograph a drawer, shelf or bin and AI picks out what's in it: likely names, category, color, material and any text it can read. Nothing is saved until you say so.

  • Camera or photo library
  • Edit, keep or delete each item
  • Prefer to type? Add any item by hand
Scan a QR label

Every box gets an identity

Print labels, stick one on each box or bin, and scan it with Clutterwise or the iPhone Camera. A new label sets up the container; a known one jumps straight to what's inside.

  • Ideal for moving boxes and storage bins
  • Choose the label layout that suits your boxes
  • Works for one household or a whole job
Search

Find it even when you forgot the name

Search the way you'd describe it. Clutterwise matches on names, details, categories and where things are stored, so a close match turns up even when the words don't line up exactly.

  • Normal search plus smarter Advanced Search
  • See where it lives: room, spot and container
  • Shows why each result matched

How it works

Capture. Organize. Find.

  1. Capture

    Scan a spot with AI, or type items in by hand. Review everything before it's saved.

  2. Organize

    File items by room, location and container. Add QR labels to the boxes and bins that matter.

  3. Find

    Search by name or description and see exactly where it lives, even with a fuzzy memory.

Features

Made for real clutter

  • AI scan or manual entry. Use whichever is faster for the job.
  • Rooms, locations and containers. Organize the way your space is actually arranged.
  • QR labels. Print them for boxes, bins and shelves; scan with Clutterwise or the Camera app.
  • Rich item details. Likely names, category, color, shape, material and text on the item.
  • Smart search. Standard search plus Advanced Search that matches on meaning and storage context.
  • Backup and restore. Export your inventory, with or without photos, and bring it back anytime.
  • Merge inventories. Bring in someone else's data without replacing your own.
  • You stay in control. Review, edit, keep or delete every item before it's saved.
