How it works

Bite, confirm, celebrate

  1. Your child takes a bite

    The bot waits. Until there's a bite, he stays asleep.

  2. You confirm it

    Hold the button on your phone, or tap BITE! on your Apple Watch.

  3. The bot celebrates

    Your child taps the bot and he puts on a show, with sound effects.

Apple Watch

BiteyBot on your wrist

The watch app comes with BiteyBot, with nothing extra to download. Install BiteyBot on your iPhone and it appears on your paired Apple Watch. The screen is just two big buttons, so you can use it with your eyes on your kid.

  1. BITE! (top)

    Tap it when your child takes a bite. It turns green and says "Bot is ready!" and stays that way until your child taps the bot, then goes back to BITE!. It always matches what your phone shows.

  2. Encourage (bottom)

    Tap it to send the bot over for a bit of encouragement. It says "Sent!" once your phone has it. Keep BiteyBot open on your iPhone for this one.

  3. Vibration only

    Every tap gives a quick vibration and nothing else, with no sounds to distract your child. If your iPhone is out of reach, the watch holds your bite for a moment and tells you it's waiting.

When they need a nudge

The bot does the encouraging

No bite yet?

If your child taps him before taking a bite, the bot shakes his head with a cute "uh-uh" sound.

He wakes up on his own

When things go quiet, the bot walks up and asks for a bite. Pick how often: every 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds.

Keeps trying

A few more taps without a bite, and he'll cheer your child on to take one.

Call him over

Tap Encourage on your Apple Watch and the bot comes over, no need to touch the phone.

Features

Features

  • A bot who loves his food. He tucks into a sandwich, a hamburger or a hot dog, with a taco, a burrito, pizza, spaghetti, oatmeal and more, and he drinks water too.
  • Apple Watch companion. Confirm a bite with one tap on your wrist, even when your hands are full. It only vibrates, so there's nothing to distract the kids.
  • Choose his tricks. Eating, tickle, or everything, from backflips to flying around the screen.
  • Free play. Switch off the bite requirement whenever you just want to play.
  • Parent controls. Hide the hold button if your child figures it out, and turn the encouragement on or off.
  • Sound you control. Mute everything with one switch.
  • Private. No ads, no account, no tracking, and it works offline.
