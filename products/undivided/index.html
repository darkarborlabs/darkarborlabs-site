Undivided

Lifestyle

Divide the things, not the family.

Deciding who gets what is one of the hardest parts of a family's loss, and it's often left unsaid until it's too late. Undivided lets you set aside keepsakes with the stories behind them, choose who each is for, and hand your family and your attorney a clear record. Everything stays on your phone, with no account and no ads.
