ClipStitch

Combine Videos

Pick your clips, drag them into order, and export one seamless video. No timeline, no learning curve.

The founder's daughter loves making toy play videos, but complex video editing apps got in her way. The clear gap was an easy way to combine clips in the order you want them. ClipStitch is perfect for her, and we hope for you!

How it works

Select, order, stitch

  1. Select your videos

    Choose the clips you want from your library.

  2. Drag into order

    Use the handle on each clip to put them in the order you like.

  3. Tap Stitch

    ClipStitch combines them into one video and saves it for you.

Features

Simple on purpose

  • Your order, your way. Drag clips up and down, or remove the ones you don't want.
  • Pick the quality. Export in 720p, 1080p or 4K, with a "best match" suggestion so you never upscale by accident.
  • Mixed clips are fine. Combine portrait and landscape clips and choose the shape of the final video.
  • Know the size first. See an estimated file size before you export.
  • Keeps going in the background. Leave the app and get a notification when your video is ready.
  • Private. Your videos are processed on your device and never uploaded.
