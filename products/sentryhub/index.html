Sentry Hub

Utilities

Your Frigate cameras, in a native app.

Sentry Hub is a native client for Frigate NVR on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. Watch live video, scrub a timeline of recordings, get smart alerts, and let face recognition tell family from strangers. Your cameras stay on your own server, and a built-in demo lets you look around without one.

  • Home Assistant buttons. Add quick actions to any camera to turn on a light, unlock a door, or open a gate, without leaving the live view.
  • Built-in WireGuard tunnel. Sentry Hub checks whether your server is reachable on your home network and, if not, brings up your own WireGuard tunnel for you, then shuts it down when you leave the app. No separate VPN app to juggle.
  • Face-aware alerts. Silence household members, get a custom chime when a VIP arrives, and escalate when an unknown person lingers.
