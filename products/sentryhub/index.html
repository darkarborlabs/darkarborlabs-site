Sentry Hub

Utilities

Your Frigate cameras, native on Apple.

Sentry Hub is a native client for Frigate NVR on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Watch live video, scrub a timeline of recordings, get smart alerts, and let face recognition tell family from strangers. Your cameras stay on your own server, and a built-in demo lets you look around without one.
