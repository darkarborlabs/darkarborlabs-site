PickiBot

Spinner Wheel Picker

Can't decide where to eat, who goes first, or what to watch? Build a wheel, tap spin, and let a friendly robot pick for you.

How it works

Add, spin, pick

  1. Add your options

    Type in anything you like, from as few as two choices up.

  2. Tap SPIN

    The wheel spins, with sound effects you can mute.

  3. PickiBot picks

    The robot announces the winner.

Two ways to play

Two play modes

Pick One mode

Spin once and the wheel picks the winner.

Elimination mode

Each spin knocks one option out until only one is left.

Features

Features

  • Save as many wheels as you like. "Dinner tonight", "Who does the dishes?", "Game night".
  • A Yes / No wheel is included for the quickest decisions.
  • Play without saving when it's a one-off choice.
  • An interactive robot. Tap him and he reacts, or hide him if you only want the wheels.
  • Sound you control. Mute everything with a single tap.
  • Private. Your wheels stay on your device, and the app never goes online.
