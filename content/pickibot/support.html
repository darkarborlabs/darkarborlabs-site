PickiBot collects nothing and doesn't connect to the internet, so there's nothing to sign up for and nothing leaving your device.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email pickibot@darkarborlabs.com.

Your data

Your wheels and settings are stored only on your device. To remove them, uninstall PickiBot or clear the app's data in your device settings. Read the privacy policy for details.
