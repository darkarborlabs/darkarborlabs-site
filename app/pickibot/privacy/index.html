PickiBot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Publisher: Dark Arbor Labs LLC

Contact: pickibot@darkarborlabs.com

PickiBot is a spinner-wheel app. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

What PickiBot collects

Nothing. PickiBot does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

What stays on your device

The wheels and options you create, plus your settings (play mode, sound on/off, show/hide the robot), are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. Dark Arbor Labs LLC never receives this data. It is deleted when you uninstall PickiBot or clear the app's data in your device settings.

If you use your device's backup (such as iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, or Android's device backup), your device may include this app data in your own backup under your Apple or Google account's terms. That is controlled by you and Apple or Google, not by us.

App stores

PickiBot is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information about installs and your use of their stores under Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information from Apple or Google about you.

Children

PickiBot is suitable for all ages. It isn't designed specifically for children, but it collects no data from anyone, including children, so there is nothing to collect, share, or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
