Helpful to include

  • What you were doing and what happened instead
  • Your device model and its iOS or Android version
  • The ClipStitch version, if you know it (Settings shows it)
  • How many clips you were combining and the quality you chose
  • A screenshot, if it helps

Quick answers

Where is my finished video? On Android it's in Movies/ClipStitch. On iPhone and iPad it's in your Photos library.

Why can I only add 2 clips? That's the free version's limit. ClipStitch Pro has unlimited clips and exports up to 4K.

My export stopped when I left the app. Keep notifications on so ClipStitch can keep working in the background, and tell us your device and clip lengths if it still stops.
